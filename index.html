What we build

We build product ventures, with fine fragrance and cosmetics as our most advanced category, and support selected mandates in DTC commerce and brand systems.

01

Fragrance & Cosmetics Development

Concept-to-formula development in partnership with fragrance houses, independent perfumers and cosmetic formulation partners, targeting fine fragrance (EDP) concentration and modern olfactive positioning.

02

Product & Packaging Systems

Format and packaging development across pocket, standard, and refillable presentations, built for both retail shelf and direct-to-consumer channels.

03

DTC Market Readiness

Aligning product architecture, compliance readiness, commercial positioning and direct-to-consumer channel requirements as concepts progress from prototype development toward market entry.

How we work

Clear scopes, direct decisions and documented next steps keep development focused and allow selected partners to move quickly.

Direct decision-making

Remote operating model

BLUE MAGIC LABS operates as a remote-first consumer product and venture development company. Strategy, supplier development, fragrance creation, packaging, DTC commerce and AI-assisted operating systems are coordinated digitally through structured documentation, direct supplier communication and selected strategic partnerships.

International collaboration

Work is organised across time zones through scheduled video conferences, written briefs and shared documentation, with decisions recorded at each stage.

Supplier coordination

Fragrance houses, glass, component, packaging and filling partners are briefed, qualified under NDA and managed through clear specifications and direct correspondence.

Venture development

Internal product ventures are built from brand architecture and concept through prototype, partner selection and market preparation.

DTC systems

Direct-to-consumer architecture, channel strategy and launch planning for internal ventures and selected commerce strategy mandates, grounded in European and GCC market experience.

Digital product systems

Websites, brand systems, investor materials and internal tooling developed as one documented system rather than as separate deliverables.

AI-assisted operations

AI-assisted research, documentation and workflow design, used internally to make product and venture development faster and clearer.
