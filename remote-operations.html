Remote Operations

An international operating model, coordinated digitally.

How work is coordinated

Each project follows the same documented sequence, whether it involves a fragrance house, a packaging supplier or a DTC launch.

01

Brief

Objectives, scope and constraints are defined in writing before work begins.

02

Qualification

Partners are assessed and, where specifications are shared, engaged under NDA.

03

Development

Work progresses through scheduled video conferences, email correspondence and versioned documents.

04

Review & handover

Decisions, specifications and next steps are recorded and shared with the relevant partners.

Digital infrastructure

Communication runs through company-domain email and scheduled video conferences. Briefs, specifications and decision logs are maintained in shared cloud workspaces, with access limited to the partners involved in each project.

Communication

Company-domain email for all partner correspondence, with scheduled video conferences for reviews and decisions.

Documentation

Versioned briefs, specifications and decision logs, kept as the single reference for each project.

Access

Project materials are shared on a need-to-know basis, and confidential specifications only after an NDA is in place.
